Mandarin Lessons

Online Mandarin Lessons in Malaysia

Private and small group Mandarin lessons for children and adults, with practical support for speaking, pronunciation, Pinyin and reading.

What we can work on

Speaking

Build confidence through guided conversation, questions and familiar daily topics.

Pronunciation

Work carefully on tones, sound differences and clearer spoken Mandarin.

Pinyin

Use Pinyin as a practical bridge between listening, pronunciation and independent reading.

Reading

Develop word recognition and sentence understanding through level-appropriate material.

Who these lessons are for

  • Children who need structured support alongside school learning.
  • Learners who can read or write some Chinese but feel shy speaking.
  • Adults who want practical Mandarin for daily communication.
  • Small groups of 2–4 students learning at a similar level.

How learning begins

Tell me your goal

Share the learner's age, current ability and whether the priority is speaking, reading or school support.

Check the foundation

We identify familiar words, pronunciation habits and areas that need support.

Practise actively

Lessons combine clear explanation with speaking, listening and reading practice suited to the learner.
