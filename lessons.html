What to Expect from My Lessons

Every student starts from a different place, so here's a little about how lessons actually work — not just what's on the syllabus.

  • Built around where you are. Lessons start from your current level and what you actually want to use the language for — not a fixed, one-size-fits-all plan.
  • Focused on real communication. The goal is language you can actually use — speaking and understanding that holds up outside the lesson, not just on paper.
  • Paced to you. Some weeks move quickly, some slower. We adjust as we go, based on what's actually working for you.
  • A mix of structure and practice. Enough structure to keep things moving forward, enough real conversation to make it stick.
  • A comfortable place to get things wrong. Asking questions and making mistakes are part of how this works — there's no pressure to get everything right straight away.
