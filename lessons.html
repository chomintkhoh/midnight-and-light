How lessons work

  • Built around you. We start from your level and goals, and go at your pace.

  • Mistakes are welcome. No pressure to get everything right straight away.
Booking & cancellation
  • Please pay before the lesson to confirm your slot.
  • Change the time with at least 24 hours' notice. Later cancellations count as a lesson taken.
  • Small groups need at least 2 students. If only one can come, the lesson becomes 1-to-1 (RM90) or can be moved with 24 hours' notice.
