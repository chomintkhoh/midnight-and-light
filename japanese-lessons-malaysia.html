Japanese Lessons

Online Japanese Lessons in Malaysia

Private and small group Japanese lessons for children and adults, from complete beginner foundations to conversation, reading and JLPT preparation.

What we can work on

Beginner foundations

Start with greetings, Hiragana, useful vocabulary and simple sentence patterns in a clear order.

Conversation

Practise speaking through guided questions, daily situations and language you can use outside class.

Grammar & reading

Build sentences step by step and connect grammar with reading instead of memorising isolated rules.

JLPT preparation

Structured support for JLPT N5–N3 vocabulary, grammar, reading and revision.

Who these lessons are for

  • Children aged around 7–8 and above who need an encouraging, structured introduction.
  • Adult beginners who want a clear place to start.
  • Learners who know some Japanese but need more speaking and sentence-building practice.
  • Students preparing for JLPT N5, N4 or N3.

How learning begins

Tell me your goal

Share your age or level, previous learning and what you hope to do in Japanese.

Find your starting point

We identify what you already know and choose a suitable pace and focus.

Learn and use it

Lessons combine explanation, guided practice and communication, with review where needed.
