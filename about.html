About

A little about the person behind
Midnight & Light.

I am a Japanese and Mandarin tutor based in Malaysia.

My background brings together language teaching, cross-cultural communication, coordination, administration, and business development in Japanese-related environments. Working across different languages, cultures, and industries has shaped the way I approach communication and learning.

I believe a language is not simply something to memorise. It is something we gradually learn to understand, use, and make our own.

I currently offer private and small group Japanese and Mandarin lessons, both online and in person. I teach in Japanese and Mandarin, and communicate with students in Chinese, Japanese, Cantonese, and basic English.
