My Teaching Approach

My teaching focuses on building a strong and stable foundation. Many students already have exposure to the language, but still feel uncertain when using it.


We take a step-by-step approach to rebuild clarity in:

• Sentence structure

• Pronunciation habits

• Core language patterns used in real life

This is a structured learning process designed for long-term stability, not quick results.
