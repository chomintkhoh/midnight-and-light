The path

Your Journey from
Beginner to Confident Speaker

Learn the Japanese you need to survive daily life. Short sentences. High-frequency phrases. Real contexts.

  • Greet people naturally and respond to greetings
  • Introduce yourself and ask simple questions
  • Order food and drinks confidently
  • Count, ask prices, handle basic transactions
  • Understand slow, clear spoken Japanese

Go deeper. Longer conversations, connected sentences, more complex scenarios. Start to think in Japanese.

  • Hold a back-and-forth conversation on familiar topics
  • Describe people, places, and situations
  • Handle unexpected responses in a conversation
  • Express preferences, opinions, and simple feelings
  • Navigate school, shopping, travel, and social settings

Move from scripted situations to open conversation. The training wheels come off. Handle real, unpredictable dialogue.

  • Speak with natural rhythm and response speed
  • Handle corrections and recover mid-conversation
  • Express complex ideas with appropriate nuance
  • Understand native-speed Japanese in context
  • Carry a genuine conversation with a Japanese speaker
