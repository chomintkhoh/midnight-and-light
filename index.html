The Path

Your Journey from
Beginner to Fluent

Short sentences. High-frequency phrases. Real contexts. The Japanese you need to survive daily life.

  • Greet people and respond naturally
  • Introduce yourself and ask simple questions
  • Order food and drinks confidently
  • Handle basic transactions and prices
  • Understand slow, clear spoken Japanese

Longer conversations. Connected sentences. You start to think in Japanese rather than translate.

  • Hold a back-and-forth conversation
  • Describe people, places, and situations
  • Handle unexpected responses
  • Express preferences and opinions
  • Navigate school, shopping, travel settings

Open conversation. Unpredictable dialogue. The training wheels come off.

  • Speak with natural rhythm and speed
  • Recover confidently mid-conversation
  • Express complex ideas with nuance
  • Understand native-speed Japanese
  • Hold a genuine conversation with a native speaker
